About Komaru

Komaru is a japanese internet cat, specifically a Kinkalow, known for her appearance and funny or cute interactions with the two other cats she lives with, Komugi, and Cocoa.

Komaru is posted on Instagram by her owner, mugimeshi323, and commonly clipped for GIFs or TikToks.

The most popular videos of Komaru involve her edited on top of Minecraft animations to appear as if she is inside the game playing.
